4.4 FEATURE ENGINEERING
------------------------------------------------------------
Created 5 new features to enhance analysis:

1. Total_Renewable_Power_kW
   Formula: PV_Power_kW + Wind_Power_kW
   Purpose: Aggregate renewable energy capacity

2. PV_to_Wind_Ratio
   Formula: PV_Power_kW / (Wind_Power_kW + 1)
   Purpose: Compare solar vs wind contribution

3. Efficiency_Factor
   Formula: H2_Production × System_Efficiency / 100
   Purpose: Effective hydrogen production considering efficiency

4. H2_per_kW
   Formula: H2_Production / Total_Renewable_Power
   Purpose: Hydrogen production per unit power (productivity metric)

5. Latitude_Abs
   Formula: abs(Latitude)
   Purpose: Distance from equator (for climate analysis)
